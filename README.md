# Zoo Tycoon

A goofy-physics, first-person, low-poly zoo game that runs in the browser (Three.js + cannon-es, no build step).

Sell tickets at the front gate, haul cash to the ATM, order supplies from ZooMart, feed the animals, drive the golf cart, and try not to get slapped by the monkeys.

## Play locally

```bash
python3 serve.py
```

Then open http://localhost:5173.

## Controls

- WASD to move, mouse to look, Space to jump, Shift to sprint
- Click to pick something up, click again to put it down
- Hold Q to wind up a throw, let go to throw (longer hold = harder throw)
- E to interact (computers, the ATM, the golf cart, handing tickets to visitors)
- F to use what you're holding
